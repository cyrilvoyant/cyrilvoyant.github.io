Current research lines

  • Solar and PV forecasting from minutes to days, with honest baselines and evaluation (NICE metrics, γ-index).
  • Frugal AI for energy systems: how much model is enough, Extreme Learning Machines, deseasonalization.
  • Energy management of PV–battery systems and microgrids.
  • Research assessment and research excellence (see publications).
  • Medical physics: radiotherapy dose equivalence (LQL-Equiv), radioiodine therapy modelling.
