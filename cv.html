Cyril Voyant — Curriculum vitae

Updated 2026-10-18

Physicist and Research Director at Mines Paris–PSL, Centre Observation, Impacts, Énergie (O.I.E.), Sophia Antipolis, and Visiting Professor at the University of Kragujevac. I study solar forecasting, time series, parsimonious AI and forecast evaluation, with a background in medical physics. ORCID 0000-0003-0242-7377.

Selected publications, open software and live tools: methods & code · live tools · full list on HAL and Google Scholar.
