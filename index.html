Open-source Software

Make_Stationary

ELM-based cyclic deseasonalization for PV/GHI with phase-conditioned projection. JSON-LD & CFF included.

ARTU

Benchmark utilities for solar forecasting (aligned with Renewable Energy, 2022).

Data_Viz_2024

MATLAB figures for hourly energy production, diurnal/annual cycles, residual diagnostics.

DICOM-Transfer-Modify

Deterministic JPEG/J2K → Explicit VR LE DICOM pipeline (DCMTK/GDCM) incl. anonymisation + PACS.

LQ-Equiv

Radiotherapy dose-equivalence calculator based on LQ/LQL models.
