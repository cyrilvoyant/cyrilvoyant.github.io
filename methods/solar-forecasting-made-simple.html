Principle 2 · The model at the coreThe approach

In the story: this page maps the methods onto the ladder, understand the signal, beat simple references, then add tools only if they pay.

A useful solar forecast starts with the right target, a meaningful reference and an evaluation that reflects the application. Additional model complexity must earn its place.

This guide connects my work and that of my colleagues. The linked studies provide the full author lists, evidence, limitations and code.

Start with the signal and its forecastability

Day and night form a predictable cycle; clouds are less regular. Separating these components helps ask how much information remains beyond the recurring cycle.

Our team's published contribution: the stochastic coefficient of variation, sCV, and associated forecastability indices. Their interpretation depends on their definitions and evaluation conditions.

Require a baseline before adding complexity

Persistence is an established idea: carry recent information forward. Define it clearly and avoid an artificially weak reference.

Our team's published contribution: BLEND uses the cyclic statistical structure of energy series to build an analytical reference forecast. This does not establish universal superiority over all models or sites.

Measure what the forecast improves

An isolated error score does not always show whether a model improves on a useful reference.

Our team's published contribution: NICEᵏ compares error components with explicit baselines. Interpret each quantity using its formula, identical observations and a common horizon. It does not measure economic value by itself.

NICE study and coauthors · Definitions and implementation

Distinguish value errors from displacement

A slightly displaced cloud structure can receive a large pointwise penalty. Whether displacement is acceptable depends on the application.

Published contribution: my forecast-validation γ-index combines spatial, temporal and intensity tolerances. It adapts tolerance-based evaluation to forecasting; it does not claim the invention of the gamma-index in medical physics.
